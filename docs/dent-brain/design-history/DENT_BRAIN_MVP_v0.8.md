## Problem Statement

v0.7 is architecturally sound but sized for an 11-phase build before any team member touches the system. Steve Broback is available to alpha now. The primary risk identified in this office-hours session is **team adoption**, not architecture — the plan needs to get a real user doing real work as fast as possible, not build nine phases of machinery speculatively. MVP v0.8 keeps the core value prop of v0.7 (compiled entity pages with typed sections, not raw timelines) and cuts the parts that don't need to exist before Steve validates the shape: stream ingestors, intelligence skills, dream-cycle, server-side Dropbox cron, Gmail scheduled ingest, Granola ingest.

**On hallucination risk:** LLM-synthesized compiled-truth sections are kept in MVP. The alternative — timeline-only pages — ships a log, not a brain. Compiled truth is the token-efficient surface that makes day-to-day queries ("did we pitch Mike?") fast and useful. The risk of hallucinated confirmed facts is accepted for MVP with guardrails: mandatory source-tag backlinks on every synthesized claim, `/flag-fact` skill for fast correction, and a correction log that drives prompt iteration. The mechanical corroboration rubric discussed in this session (structured claims corroborated by ≥2 evidence records) remains the post-MVP hardening path — we tighten the authority surface once Steve has flagged enough bad facts to give us a calibration signal.

## Premises

These are the load-bearing assumptions. Each was challenged in this office hours session.

1. **The evidence-log-first architecture from v0.7 is correct.** Append-only evidence records with typed entity views solve the multi-writer, imports-blow-up-mutable-designs, compiled-truth-hides-uncertainty problems. Agreed.

2. **Compiled truth ships in MVP with guardrails; mechanical hardening comes later.** The token-efficiency and day-to-day utility of compiled entity pages is too valuable to defer. MVP uses LLM synthesis for confirmed facts, working inferences, and conflict detection. Guardrails: every synthesized claim carries source-tag backlinks, `/flag-fact` enables one-click correction, and a correction log drives prompt iteration. Post-MVP hardening path: migrate confirmed-facts to mechanical extraction (structured claims corroborated by ≥2 evidence records, no LLM prose in the authority surface) once correction-log data tells us where the prompts fail. Revised this session after pushback.

3. **Steve is enough to validate MVP.** One real user doing real Dent work is worth more than 11 phases of machinery. If Steve uses it and wants Jeff on it, we expand. If Steve bounces off it, we learn why before building more. Agreed.

4. **Proactive `/append-evidence` suggestion is core, not optional.** Asking users to remember to invoke a skill is a losing pattern. The signal-detector skill that reads Cowork session context and suggests the append is the actual UX. Added this session.

5. **Materializer synthesis is in MVP.** Confirmed facts, working inferences, open questions, conflicts, and timeline all ship in MVP. Synthesis is LLM-driven. Guardrails enumerated in premise 2. Hallucination risk is accepted and Steve is the primary detector via `/flag-fact`. Revised this session.

6. **Bulk Dropbox import must happen before MVP ships to Steve.** An empty brain is not testable. The import is also the first real stress-test of classification and entity detection. Agreed.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Ship v0.7 as written, with corrections
Push through all 11 phases before first user contact. Correct the confirmed-facts synthesis mechanism along the way.
- Pros: complete system at end.
- Cons: ~3 months of dark work before Jeff/Steve touch anything. High risk that the shape is wrong for actual Dent workflow. Signal-detector pattern not in scope until very late.

### Approach B: Ship narrowest useful wedge to Steve (RECOMMENDED, revised)
Evidence log + LLM-synthesized compiled entity pages (confirmed facts, inferences, open questions, conflicts, timeline) + Dropbox bulk import + signal-detector + `/append-evidence` + `/flag-fact` + non-technical install. No streams, no intelligence skills, no dream cycle, no server-side cron ingest. Steve on it in ~3-4 focused build weeks.
- Pros: real user validates real behavior on the actual product (compiled brain, not a log). Signal-detector ships as core MVP UX. Correction log from `/flag-fact` directly drives the post-MVP mechanical-corroboration hardening.
- Cons: hallucination risk in confirmed-facts is accepted, mitigated by source-tag backlinks + `/flag-fact`. Materializer cost needs monitoring as evidence density grows. Dropbox classification quality is still the primary ingest-failure surface.

### Approach C: Notion/Airtable substrate
Skip custom evidence log entirely. Use Notion as the data layer; skills read/write Notion API.
- Pros: fastest to ship. Free UI for team.
- Cons: kills gstack-substrate thesis and portability story. Tied to Notion's performance ceiling. Migration cost later is high. Not interesting to build.

## Recommended Approach: B

**Why:** v0.7's architecture is right. The risk isn't architectural, it's adoption and ingest-quality. Approach B puts both in front of Steve as fast as possible while shipping the actual value prop (compiled entity pages, not a log). v0.7's typed-sections design is preserved in MVP; the change is that LLM synthesis runs the sections instead of mechanical rules. Mechanical rules become a v1 hardening pass driven by `/flag-fact` data. Nothing in MVP v0.8 contradicts v0.7's long-term targets.

## MVP Scope (concrete)

### IN (ship to Steve)

**Infrastructure**
- Railway deployment of dent-brain server (Bun) + Supabase (Postgres + pgvector).
- Custom subdomain `dent-brain.dentthefuture.com/mcp` with bearer auth.
- Audit log on every MCP tool call.

**Data**
- Evidence log schema per v0.7 §6 (id, source, author, timestamp, ingested_at, content, content_hash, entity_refs[], stream_id, batch_id).
- `dent-brain-data` private repo in dentthefuture GitHub org, initialized with namespace scaffolding per v0.7 §7 and `DENT_SCHEMA.md`.
- Seed entity pages for Steve, Jason, Dent itself (hand-written, to anchor entity detection).

**MCP tools (server-side)**
- `append_evidence(entity_refs, content, source_context?)`
- `get_evidence(entity_slug, limit?)`
- `get_page(slug)` — entity or namespace, renders compiled sections (confirmed facts, working inferences, open questions, conflicts, evidence timeline)
- `search(query)` — gstack's existing hybrid RRF over evidence + entity pages
- `list_recent_writes(since, user?)`
- `whoami()`
- `rebuild_entity(slug)` — rebuilds the whole compiled page from evidence: runs synthesis, writes markdown, updates search index
- `flag_fact(entity_slug, claim, reason?)` — marks a synthesized claim as wrong; demotes it on next rebuild and writes to the correction log
- `get_provenance(entity_slug)` — lists all sources and authors that contributed to a page, for "where did this come from?" queries

**Materializer (LLM-driven, MVP-grade)**
- Renders compiled sections: confirmed facts, working inferences, open questions, conflicts, evidence timeline.
- **Mandatory source-tag backlinks on every synthesized claim.** No bare prose. Pattern: `Interested in speaking at Dent 2026. [src: jason-paste:2026-04-14, jeff-paste:2026-04-15]`.
- Conflict detection is mechanical when possible: evidence records with opposing claims (e.g., "travel-constrained" vs "not travel-constrained") about the same entity go to the Conflicts section until resolved.
- Runs on `append_evidence` (enqueues a rebuild) and on-demand via `rebuild_entity`.
- Correction log captures every `flag_fact` call; weekly review drives prompt iteration.

**Ingest**
- `/ingest-dropbox-tree` — bulk import with classification, staged batches, `quarantine_batch` + `diff_entity` tooling for review.
- `/append-evidence` skill — user-invoked or signal-detector-suggested, available in Cowork.
- `/flag-fact <entity> <claim>` skill — Steve's primary mechanism for correcting hallucinated facts. One-click in Cowork.

**Proactive UX (new, this session)**
- `signal-detector-dent` — per-message skill in Cowork plugin. Reads active session context, matches against Dent entities (seed pages + entities discovered during Dropbox import), sponsor companies, conference phrases, dent-related email/domain patterns. When matched and confidence > threshold, suggests one of:
  - "I see you're analyzing Dent social media ad performance. Want me to `/append-evidence` about the effectiveness?"
  - "I see you're drafting a sponsorship PDF for {company}. Want me to `/append-evidence` about who it's being sent to?"
  - "I see you mentioned {speaker} and they're in the Dent speaker pipeline. Want me to `/append-evidence` about this conversation?"
- Rate-limit suggestions to ≤2 per session. One-click dismiss. User can `/signal-detector-dent off` to silence for a session.

**Install**
- Cowork plugin bundle: connector URL, skills bundle (`/append-evidence`, `/flag-fact`, `whoami`, `signal-detector-dent`), install walkthrough doc.
- Admin CLI for issuing per-user bearer tokens.
- Onboarding DM template for non-technical users.

**Steve onboarding**
- Admin token issued.
- Install walkthrough with Jason present (video call, one shot).
- First week: Steve uses it for real Dent work, logs friction in a shared doc.

### OUT of MVP (deferred to v1 and v1.1 per v0.7)

## Open Questions

1. **Signal-detector trigger list.** What exactly counts as a Dent signal in a Cowork session? Seed from DENT_SCHEMA.md + imported entities, but needs tuning with Steve. First week of Steve use is the eval.
2. **Dropbox classification accuracy.** v0.7 §17 sets <20% human-correction target. MVP should measure this on the staged import and block ship-to-Steve if classification is clearly broken.
3. **Materializer cost budget.** LLM synthesis on every entity rebuild has non-trivial token cost, especially as evidence density grows. Target: materializer re-run cost for a heavy entity (200+ evidence records) under $0.10 per rebuild. If it exceeds that, implement incremental synthesis (only new evidence since last rebuild) before shipping.
4. **Hallucination rate calibration.** We don't yet know Steve's `/flag-fact` rate per hundred synthesized claims. Track it from day one. If >5 flags per 100 claims by end of week 1, accelerate the mechanical-corroboration hardening plan.
5. **`/append-evidence` free-text vs structured claims.** Accepting free text for MVP is fine. When mechanical-corroboration hardening starts (post-MVP), revisit whether `/append-evidence` should emit structured claims or a claim-extraction pass runs as a separate step.
6. **Entity detection on import.** Imported files must create entity pages for people/companies/sessions not yet seeded. Heuristic: any name appearing in ≥3 files or any @dentthefuture.com or known-partner email becomes an entity. Tune after dry-run.

## Success Criteria (MVP specifically)

MVP is successful if, 2 weeks after ship-to-Steve, all of these are true:

**Adoption**
1. Steve opens Cowork at least 3 times per week and interacts with Dent Brain.
2. Steve has triggered `/append-evidence` (via signal-detector suggestion or direct invoke) at least 10 times across the two weeks.
3. Steve has queried the brain (search or entity page read) at least 20 times.

**Correctness**
4. >90% of Steve's `/append-evidence` calls land on the correct entity page (no missing entity_ref, no wrong entity).
5. Dropbox import produced entity pages for the top 20 expected entities (Jason, Steve, Jeff, Robin, Andreas, Morgan, Dent, and ~13 known speakers/sponsors).
6. Signal-detector suggestions: >50% accepted or ignored-without-annoyance, <50% dismissed as noise. Steve reports qualitatively whether the pattern helps or hurts.
7. **Hallucination rate tolerable.** `/flag-fact` calls by Steve < 5 per 100 synthesized claims read. Above that threshold, accelerate mechanical-corroboration hardening.
8. **Every synthesized claim on every entity page has at least one source-tag backlink.** Zero tolerance for bare prose in compiled sections.
9. `rebuild_entity` is reproducible enough that a second rebuild on unchanged evidence produces substantively identical compiled sections (exact prose may differ; facts and inferences must not). Formal determinism is the post-MVP mechanical rubric's job.
10. Zero silent data loss: every evidence record appended is retrievable; every `/append-evidence` call either succeeds with an ID returned or fails with an explicit error.

**Hygiene**
11. Steve can complete the install in under 15 minutes with zero terminal.
12. Jason has not had to write code to fix Steve's environment once setup is done.
13. At least one Steve quote worth quoting (good or bad) at the end of week 2.
